How matching works

Needs and wants, both valued.

Most apps score one thing. ROCK scores two and only connects people where both hold: your dating type — the formation the quiz gave you — and your rock interests.

Axis 01 — dating type

Formation compatibility

Your quiz result is a pace, not a personality. Sedimentary wants to build slowly. Igneous wants to go now. Metamorphic has been changed by pressure and is fine about it. Some pairs hold, some shear.

Igneous + igneousHolds, loudly

Questions

Before you leave an email.

Do I have to be a geologist?

No. Rock people, broadly defined — geologists, boulderers, punks, cave swimmers. One of those is enough.

What does the quiz actually measure?

Pace and pressure, not taste. It returns a rock type — sedimentary, igneous, or metamorphic — that describes how you date. Interests are scored separately.

Can I change my rock type later?

You can retake the quiz every 30 days, so take it seriously. No gaming the system.

When does it open?

As soon as we have enough people on the waitlist. Tell your friends!

What happens to my email?

It sits on the waitlist and gets used once, at launch. No newsletter, no sharing it with anyone else.

Is this a rock music app or a geology app?

Both, on purpose. The interest layer keeps them apart when you want them apart.
